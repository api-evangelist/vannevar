# Vannevar Labs

> Vannevar Labs is a defense technology company building agentic AI for national security missions. Its Agent Orchestration platform combines defense-grade foundational data collected from adversarial information environments with mission-focused AI agents — course-of-action, force-protection, information-operations, and cross-border pattern-analysis agents — supporting decisions from the tactical kill chain to theater-level campaigns. Vannevar Labs publishes no public developer portal or API.
